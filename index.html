FactFlow

FactFlow is a personal tool that makes short, sourced science videos and uploads them to its owner's own YouTube channel.

Each video explains one science or nature question. Every fact in a video comes from a reputable source, such as a space agency, museum or university, and the sources are listed in the video description.

How it uses YouTube

FactFlow signs in with Google only to:

  • upload videos to the owner's channel,
  • read the channel's basic details, and
  • read view and watch-time statistics for the videos it uploaded.

FactFlow is not offered to the public. Only the channel owner uses it.
